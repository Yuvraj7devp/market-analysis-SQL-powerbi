📌 Project Overview

Market Analysis is a customer and product intelligence analytics project designed to uncover meaningful patterns in online grocery purchasing behavior.

The project integrates multiple datasets including orders, products, aisles, departments, and order-product relationships to analyze how customers purchase products, which products are most frequently ordered or reordered, how order behavior changes throughout the day and week, and how products are distributed across departments and aisles.

SQL was used to perform data manipulation, aggregation, filtering, and multi-table joins. The resulting analysis was visualized through an interactive Power BI dashboard to make the insights easier to interpret from a business perspective.

The project focuses on answering key business questions around:

- Customer purchasing behavior
- Product popularity and performance
- Reorder and repeat-purchase behavior
- Department and aisle performance
- Order timing and demand patterns
- Order-size distribution
- Customer reach across departments

  # 💡 Key Business Insights

- The dataset contains 63.1K unique users, indicating a substantial customer base for behavioral analysis.
- Customers purchase an average of 10.53 products per order, highlighting relatively large basket sizes.
- 59.85% overall reorder rate indicates strong repeat-purchase behavior across the analyzed products.
- Banana is the most frequently ordered product, followed by products such as Organic Bananas, Organic Strawberries, Organic Baby Spinach, and Large Lemon.
- Produce has the highest customer reach among departments, followed by Dairy & Eggs and Beverages.
- Order activity varies significantly by hour, with demand concentrated during the daytime and peaking around the late morning.
- Reorder rates vary considerably across aisles, with milk, water seltzer sparkling water, fresh fruits, and eggs among the highest-performing aisles in the analysis.
- The order-size distribution shows that smaller baskets are more common, with the frequency of orders generally declining as the number of products per order increases.

- 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies can be considered:

1. Prioritize high-reorder products
   - Maintain strong availability of frequently reordered products to reduce the risk of stockouts.

2. Use customer behavior for targeted marketing
   - Develop personalized promotions around products and departments with strong repeat-purchase behavior.

3. Optimize inventory planning
   - Use order-hour and day-of-week patterns to anticipate periods of higher customer demand.

4. Promote complementary products
   - Analyze frequently purchased products and departments to develop cross-selling and bundle opportunities.

5. Focus on high-engagement departments
   - Departments with higher customer reach can be prioritized for promotions, product recommendations, and retention campaigns.

6. Leverage reorder behavior
   - Customers showing recurring purchase patterns can be targeted with timely reminders or personalized recommendations.
